How it works

Gaari Bazaar connects buyers and sellers directly. Documents and registration status are reported by the seller — always confirm them yourself before you pay.

01

Seller posts the listing

Photos, price, area, and the status they report — custom paid, NCP, or unregistered — go up on the site or via WhatsApp.

02

Buyer gets in touch

Interested buyers message the seller directly through the number listed — no middleman, no added fees at this stage.

03

They meet and settle it themselves

Buyer and seller inspect the vehicle, check the paperwork, and agree on price between themselves — same as any local car bazaar.
