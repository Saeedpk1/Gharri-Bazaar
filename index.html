How it works

Gaari Bazaar connects buyers and sellers directly — that's it.

1

Seller posts the listing

Photos, price, area, and the status they report — custom paid, NCP, or unregistered.

2

Buyer gets in touch

Interested buyers message the seller directly — no middleman, free at this stage.

3

They settle it themselves

Buyer and seller inspect the vehicle and agree on price directly — same as any local bazaar.

ⓘ Gaari Bazaar is a listing platform only. We do not verify registration status, documents, or ownership — always confirm these yourself before any payment.
